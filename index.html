Frequently asked questions

Planning your first HackGDG? Start here.

When and where is HackGDG?

HackGDG is planned for March 2027 at UT Dallas. We’ll post the exact dates, room, and check-in details on this page.

What happens at a hackathon?

You pick a problem or idea, build a project in a set amount of time, and share what you made. A small prototype is a great place to start.

Do I need coding experience?

Design, research, testing, and presenting are all ways to contribute to a project. You can start with the skills you already have.

Do I need a team or an idea ahead of time?

You can start brainstorming on your own. The Hackerpack will explain team sizes and how to meet other participants.

When can I sign up?

We’ll publish the registration link and deadlines here as soon as they’re available.
